The Heritage

TIMELESS BY DESIGN.EXCEPTIONAL BY NATURE.

In the winter of 1874, in a workshop above the Vallée de Joux, Élie Chroné finished his first movement by candlelight. It kept time to within two seconds a day — an obsession that has never left the house.

Five generations later, nothing essential has changed. Every CHRONÉ movement is still assembled by one watchmaker, start to finish. Every case is still polished against felt wheels until it holds light the way still water does. What has changed is the precision — and the quiet certainty that it will outlive us all.

Vallée de Joux — 1,004m

Born of Altitude & Silence

Where the air is thin and winters are long, patience becomes a discipline. The valley shaped our rhythm: slow hands, exact eyes, work that answers only to time itself.
